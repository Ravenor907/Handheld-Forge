## What's new in 0.10.0

**Downloads no longer fill up your storage.** A system image is several GB, and before this version every one you downloaded stayed on your phone or computer until you deleted it yourself. Now the app tidies up on its own, on Android and on the desktop app alike:

- **Once your boot drive is made, its download is deleted.** Want to make more drives later without downloading again? Turn on **Keep the download** before you erase the drive.
- **An image you haven't used for 14 days is deleted**, unless you chose to keep it.
- **A newer version replaces the old one**, so you never have two copies of the same system.
- **Half-finished downloads and other leftovers are cleared** when the app opens. A download you paused recently is kept, so it still picks up where it left off.
- **Downloaded images** on the main screen shows how much space they use, with a **Keep** switch for each one and a **Remove all downloaded images** button.

Each time something is cleaned up, the app tells you how much space it freed. Nothing you need is lost: anything deleted is downloaded again the next time you make that drive.

The on/off switches now glide smoothly instead of jumping.
