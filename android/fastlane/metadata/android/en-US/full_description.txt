Plug a USB stick into your phone. Tap a system. Go get a snack. Come back to a boot drive for your handheld PC.

Handheld Forge makes bootable USB drives for handhelds like the Steam Deck, ROG Ally and Legion Go, right from your phone. No PC, no root, no terminal.

<b>What it does</b>
• One-tap systems: Bazzite (Handheld KDE), SteamOS (Valve's recovery image) and CachyOS Handheld, downloaded fresh from the source.
• Your own file: any ISO or disk image on your phone, even .xz, .gz, .bz2 or .zip.
• Ventoy: one stick, every OS. Installs the latest Ventoy, with Secure Boot support on or off.
• Add images to Ventoy straight from your phone. Compressed ones are unpacked on the way in.
• Starts on its own: mark an image and Ventoy boots it with no buttons pressed, for handhelds whose controls don't work in Ventoy's menu.
• Format drive: turns a used boot stick back into a normal exFAT drive.

<b>Checked twice</b>
Downloads are checked against the publisher's checksum and signature where they publish one, and every byte is read back off the stick before it says it's done.

<b>Fast and stubborn</b>
Unpacks on every CPU core while the stick writes. Dropped Wi-Fi resumes where it left off, and a flaky adapter gets smaller, slower writes instead of a failure.

<b>Shows you the way</b>
When the drive is ready, it tells you how to open the boot menu on your handheld and how to get through the installer.

<b>You'll need</b>
A phone with USB host (OTG) support, a USB-C adapter or a USB-C stick, and about 10 GB free on the phone for the download.
